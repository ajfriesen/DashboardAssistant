# Dashboard Assistant 1.2.0: remote access over VNC

Some jobs are hard to do on a wall-mounted touchscreen, like signing in to
Google Calendar so a calendar page can show your events. With 1.2.0 you can
switch on remote access from Home Assistant and do them from your computer,
with your own keyboard and mouse.

<!-- more -->

## Remote access over VNC

Every device now has a **VNC** switch and a **VNC timeout** in Home Assistant.
Turn the switch on, and its attributes tell you where to connect and the
password to use. Connect with a VNC app and you see the device's screen and
control it from your computer.

It's built to stay out of the way when you don't need it:

- **Off by default.** VNC is off until you switch it on, and always off again
  after the device restarts. Nothing listens for VNC connections while it is
  off.
- **Closes by itself.** After 15 minutes by default. Set the timeout to 0 to
  keep it on until you switch it off.
- **A new password every time.** Each session gets its own random password,
  and Home Assistant keeps it out of its history and out of diagnostics
  downloads.
- **Encrypted only.** The device refuses VNC apps that can't encrypt the
  connection. TigerVNC, RealVNC Viewer and Remmina work; the Screen Sharing app
  built into macOS doesn't.
- **Visible.** While VNC is on, the bottom bar shows a red **● Remote access**
  marker, so anyone in front of the device knows the screen may be watched.

The VNC entities need the Dashboard Assistant integration 1.2.0 or newer.
[Remote access](../../about/use-cases.md#remote-access) has the details.

## Fixes

- **Updating from 1.0.0 and 1.1.x works.** Updates from those releases could
  fail before installing. A device on 1.0.0 or 1.1.x now downloads 1.2.0 and
  installs it without building anything itself.
- **The device-info button shows its icon.** On some devices the button on
  the bottom bar showed a missing-character box instead.

## Updating

Install the update from Home Assistant, or flash a fresh image from
[Getting Started](../../getting-started.md). The full list of changes is in the
[release notes on GitHub](https://github.com/ajfriesen/DashboardAssistant/releases/tag/v1.2.0).
